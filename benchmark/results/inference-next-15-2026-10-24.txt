Lengths 64/128/256/512, 4 warm forwards then 21 samples per length,
GC before each timed forward; compilation and initial cache construction excluded.
No concurrent benchmarks or tests from this work ran during these measurements.
Aggregate = sum of per-length medians. Speedup = 100*(baseline/current-1).
Every output checked with atol=2e-5, rtol=2e-5; precision remains Float32.

CPU aggregate: 116.942129 ms -> 100.912999 ms; 15.8841% faster.
CUDA aggregate: 6.385967 ms -> 5.526512 ms; 15.5515% faster.

These results apply to this warmed synthetic workload and hardware. They do not
promise 15% for every length, checkpoint, cold start, CPU or GPU backend.
Metal and AMDGPU performance was not measured. The new MKL_jll dependency is
loaded lazily only by eligible Intel x86-64 single-thread CPU projections.
It does not redirect libblastrampoline; the MKL thread-local limit is restored.

Reproduce CPU (same core for both):
taskset -c 8 julia --project=/path/to/f6a26ee -t1 benchmark/inference.jl --output /tmp/cpu-before.json
taskset -c 8 julia --project=. -t1 benchmark/inference.jl --baseline /tmp/cpu-before.json --require-speedup 15
